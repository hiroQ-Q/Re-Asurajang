01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Bringing the ASURAJANG experience back.

Re:Asurajang is an independent, unofficial project aiming to make the ASURAJANG experience playable again. Development and testing are progressing step by step, starting with launching the client and connecting to a local server.
